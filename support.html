Frequently Asked Questions

How does the adoption process work?

Browse our available pets, submit an adoption request form with your details, and our team will contact you to complete the process. We deliver your pet safely to your home after approval.

Are all pets vaccinated?

Yes! All our pets are fully vaccinated, dewormed, and health-certified before adoption. We provide complete health records with every pet.

What is your return policy?

We encourage careful selection, but if you have any concerns within 7 days of adoption, please contact us immediately and we'll work with you to find a solution.

Do you offer veterinary support?

Yes! We connect you with trusted veterinarians in your area and provide post-adoption support for any health-related questions.

What areas do you deliver to?

We primarily serve Delhi NCR, but we can arrange delivery to other major cities in India. Contact us for specific delivery arrangements.

Can I visit the pets before adoption?

Yes! We encourage potential adopters to meet the pets. Contact us to schedule a visit or video call to see your chosen pet.
